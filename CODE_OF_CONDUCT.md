# Code of conduct

M87 AI Gateway is intended to be a welcoming, professional open-source project.

Participants are expected to:

- Be respectful and constructive.
- Focus feedback on ideas, code, and outcomes.
- Avoid harassment, discrimination, personal attacks, and disclosure of private information.
- Keep examples and public reports free of credentials and personal data.

Report conduct concerns privately to the project contact listed in
[SECURITY.md](SECURITY.md), identifying the report as a conduct concern.
Maintainers may remove harmful content or restrict participation. Handle reports
confidentially and share only what is needed to investigate and resolve them.

Security vulnerabilities use the separate reporting procedure in
[SECURITY.md](SECURITY.md).
